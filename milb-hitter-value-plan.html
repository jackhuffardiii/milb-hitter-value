Plan · data pipeline + static site

MiLB Hitter Value

A stats-based system that puts a surplus dollar value on every A through AAA hitter, with an audit trail from minor league line to dollars. Built so I can cite a defensible number when a questionnaire asks me to value a player.

approved 2026-10-06 spec: milb-hitter-value-spec.md
Every block has an ID. To change one, say A2 should be X, not Y and only that block gets edited. IDs are never reused or renumbered.

Problem

P1

No defensible, public way to value a minor league hitter.Questionnaires ask for exactly this; an answer with no method behind it reads as a guess.

P2

The source repo has raw MiLB stats but no ages, MLB outcomes or translations. 2025 season files froze on 2025-05-01, and some rate columns are impossible.Example: 2024 AA Jake Thompson, .324 AVG with a .075 OBP.

Who it's for

U1

Me, citing player cards and the method in MLB questionnaires, job applications and portfolio links.

U2

Front office reviewers reading the site cold. Any number must trace back to its inputs.

Architecture

In scope

S1

Data layer: repo season batting 2005–2024, MiLB 2025–2026 from the Stats API, stints aggregated, rate stats recomputed from counting stats.

S2

Player bio and acquisition: birthdate, bats, MLB debut; draft round, pick and signing bonus.Stats API /people and /draft.

S3

MLB outcomes: season batting and games by position, 2005–2026.

S4

Simplified in-house WAR: park-adjusted wOBA batting runs + baserunning runs (wSB, GIDP) + positional adjustment + replacement level. Validated against bWAR.No fielding. Baserunning added 2026-10-06; extra-bases-taken not included (needs MLB play-by-play).

S5

Environment: park factors from Stats API team home/road splits (MiLB and MLB, 2005–2026, 3-year window, regressed); league-season translation factors from matched pairs, chained to MLB.Changed from PBP during B3: PBP ends May 2025 and could not cover the 2026 snapshot.

S6

MLEs: MLB-equivalent K%, BB%, ISO, BABIP per player-season, regressed by sample size.

S7

Three models trained on 2005–2017 snapshots: P(reach MLB), E[WAR in first 6 MLB seasons | reached], ETA.Features: MLEs, age vs level, highest level, projected MLB position, trajectory.

S8

Draft prior for players with no A-or-above sample (Rookie, DSL, pre-2021 A-), flagged low confidence.

S9

Backtest on held-out 2013–2017 snapshots vs the top-100 lists published the following spring (2014–2018), plus a naive age + OPS baseline on the full population.

S10

Surplus $ model: market $/WAR with inflation, league minimum for 3 pre-arb years, arb at 40/60/80% of value, 8% discount, cash flows start at ETA.

S11

Static site: leaderboard, one card per player (stats, MLEs, P(MLB), E[WAR], ETA, surplus $ 10/50/90, top drivers), methodology page with backtest and bWAR check.

S12

2026 offseason snapshot run and published.

S13

Batted-ball context on player cards: avg and 90th pct EV, hard-hit rate, launch angle, barrel rate, where tracked (AAA 2023+, Low-A FSL parks 2021+).Display only. No effect on the projection.

S14

Batted-ball input adjustment: for tracked AAA and FSL hitters, expected ISO and BABIP from EV/LA, blended toward observed by sample size, replace the observed values before the MLE and model chain.Ships only if it passes C8.

S15

Extra features: contact and swing rate; GB/FB/LD/PU mix; speed (SB attempt rate, triples); position mix (share at SS/CF/C); progression (repeat level, in-season promotion, seasons at level); height and weight.Each group kept only if it passes C9. Added 2026-10-06.

Deliberately not in v1

X1

Pitchers.v2.

X2

Fielding in WAR.No free fielding source before 2016. Baserunning moved into S4.

X3

In-season refresh or any live backend.

X4

International signing bonus data.No free source.

X5

Similarity comps on cards.Candidate for v2.

X6

Real service-time modeling (options, Super Two).

X7

Scouting grades or prospect ranks as model inputs.Ranks are the benchmark, so they stay out of the features.

Approach

A1

Value = surplus $ over team control.Front office framing; maps straight onto questionnaire prompts.

A2

Target = P(MLB) × E[WAR in first 6 MLB seasons].Seasons stand in for control years. Simple, stated openly.

A3

Train on 2005–2017 snapshots only.Later classes have censored outcomes and would look like failures.

A4

Own simplified WAR; bWAR only as a validation check.Shows the skill without the pre-2016 fielding rabbit hole.

A5

MLEs via matched pairs at league-season grain plus park, chained to MLB.Handles PCL parks and era shifts (2021 restructure, ABS, pitch clock) with no separate era logic.

A6

Logistic/linear baselines vs LightGBM, pick per model by backtest. SHAP for per-player drivers.Explainability is the requirement, accuracy second.

A7

Uncertainty via quantile models or empirical residuals, carried to a 10/50/90 surplus $ range.

A8

Python: pandas + DuckDB, parquet intermediates, scikit-learn + LightGBM. Pipeline writes JSON; static HTML/JS site renders it.Free hosting, standard front-office toolkit.

A9

Offseason snapshot, rerun by hand. No scheduler.

A10

Projected MLB position from a historical MiLB-to-MLB position transition matrix.WAR has no fielding, so position carries the defensive value; shortstops must slide down the spectrum at realistic rates.

A11

Tracking data improves inputs, not the model.No 2005–2017 training row has EV/LA, and 2021+ players have no 6-year outcomes until ~2030, so EV/LA cannot be a model feature. S14 sharpens the ISO/BABIP the trained model already uses.

A12

WAR model chosen by rank correlation among reached players in train-era CV, not RMSE.Valuation is a ranking problem. RMSE selection picked a LightGBM whose noisy ranks lost to the naive baseline in B7.

Done means

C1

Simplified WAR vs bWAR: r ≥ 0.85 on MLB player-seasons with 300+ PA; gaps explained by fielding/baserunning and documented.

C2

Backtest runs end to end on the 2013–2017 holdout and reports Spearman vs realized 6-year WAR for both model and BA rank.v1 ships whether the model wins or loses; the result is stated plainly.

C3

Model beats the naive age + OPS baseline: log loss for P(MLB), Spearman for E[WAR], full holdout.

C4

P(MLB) calibrated within 5 points per decile on holdout.

C5

Every number on a card traces to inputs on that card. One command rebuilds data, models and site JSON.

C6

2026 top 50 passes a manual smell test against current public top-100s, disagreements explained by drivers.

C7

Site is static and works at phone width with no horizontal page scroll.

C8

S14 gate: on AAA 2023–2025, expected ISO/BABIP beat observed at predicting next-season ISO/BABIP (RMSE), and AAA batted-ball metrics predict MLB results for 2023–2025 arrivals at least as well as AAA outcomes.If it fails, S14 is dropped and the methodology page reports the null result.

C9

S15 gate: a feature group is kept only if it improves train-era OOF P(MLB) log loss or ev Spearman without worsening the other.Decided before the single holdout rerun.

Assumptions I made without asking

Q1

International signees with no A-or-above sample get a population prior.They will cluster at one generic value.

Q2

"First 6 MLB seasons" approximates team control.September call-ups and demotions break the match.

Q3

Naive age + OPS baseline included alongside the top-100 benchmark.

Q4

All rate stats recomputed from counting stats; repo rate columns ignored.

Q5

Unit of analysis: one row per player per offseason snapshot. CV grouped by player.Prevents the same player landing in train and test.

Q6

Benchmark is MLB Pipeline preseason top 100 for all five years.Baseball America full lists are paywalled. The methodology page says so.

Q7

Stat projection eligibility: 150+ PA at A or above over the last two seasons, and still rookie-eligible (under 130 MLB AB).Everyone else falls to the S8 prior or is excluded.

Q8

MLB park factors computed the same way as MiLB ones, not borrowed from bWAR.

Q9

Hosting: static site deployed to Netlify after B10.User decision, 2026-10-06.

Q10

$/WAR and arb percentages pinned to cited public figures at build time.No number is fixed in this spec.

Q11

PBP downloads limited to the tracked cells B11 needs (AAA 2022+, Low-A 2021+), deleted after aggregation.

Q12

Top-100 name matching rejects ambiguous matches rather than guessing; unmatched names are listed.

Q13

Baseball Savant serves MiLB Statcast for AAA and FSL in bulk.If not, S13 and S14 fall back to repo PBP through May 2025 only.

Q14

Cards flag when S14 was applied.Tracking coverage depends on organization: at Low-A, FSL affiliates only.

Q15

Height and weight are current values, not as of each snapshot.Mild look-ahead leak, stated on the methodology page.

Q16

If 2025–2026 pitch calls cannot be pulled, contact rate for those seasons is imputed and flagged.
