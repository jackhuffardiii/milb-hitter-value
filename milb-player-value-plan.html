MiLB Player Value v2: pitchers

Pitchers join hitters on one surplus-dollar board. Both groups share a WAR scale anchored every season to Baseball-Reference's 590 hitter / 410 pitcher split, so a 2-WAR pitcher and a 2-WAR hitter are worth the same.

Every block has an ID. Comment on any block, or write S19 should be X, not Y, and only that block changes. IDs continue v1's numbering and are never reused. Anything v1 already settled and v2 doesn't amend carries over unchanged. Tags: new new in v2, gated ships only if a test passes, review filled in while drafting, not decided in the interview.

Problem

P3

v1 values only hitters, so it can't answer the most common questionnaire comparison: hitter X or pitcher Y.

P4

v1 hitter WAR uses a fixed replacement level (20 runs per 600 PA), and its season total drifts from 639 (2014) to 549 (2026). bWAR position-player WAR holds at 587 to 590 every season.On a combined board that drift would tilt hitters against pitchers depending on era.

Architecture

In scope

S17

One WAR and $ scale for hitters and pitchers. Replacement level is solved every season so position players sum to 590 WAR and pitchers to 410 per 2,430 MLB games (2020 prorated to 60 games). Hitter WAR is re-anchored and every hitter value restated.newBaseball-Reference's split, chosen over FanGraphs' 570/430 because v1 already validates against bWAR and borrows its fielding runs.

S18

Pitcher data layer: MiLB pitching 2005–2026 (plus 2000–04 history), MLB pitching 2005–2026, stints aggregated, rates recomputed from counts.new

S19

Pitcher WAR. Strikeouts, walks, HBP, and home runs are valued with v1's BaseRuns run values. Non-HR balls in play get the league-average value, plus each 2B and 3B allowed charged at its run value above a single. Park adjusted with B3 factors. Separate SP and RP replacement per appearance. Relief runs scaled by (1 + gmLI) / 2. Scaled to 410 per season.newreviewUnregressed: it measures value actually delivered, so a pitcher who really suppressed hard contact gets paid for it. The contact-term construction and the leverage formula were filled in while drafting.

S20

Pitcher MLEs: MLB-equivalent K%, BB%, HBP%, HR%, GB%, and ISO-on-contact allowed, from matched pairs, regressed toward the source league-season mean by sample size.new

S21

mERA (Majors-Equivalent Run Average): a predictive run average on the ERA scale built from MLE K%, BB%, GB%, and ISO-on-contact. Bayesian regression with level and league intercepts and level-varying slopes, partially pooled. Fit to predict next-season MLE RA9.newgatedPorted from my college pitching metric, with the exit-velocity/launch-angle contact grid replaced by ISO-on-contact, because no 2005–2017 row has EV/LA. Gamma vs Normal likelihood and varying vs fixed slopes are both picked by s ≤ 2012 CV.

S22

mERA ladder on the card: mERA at the pitcher's own level, translated to each level above it and to MLB.newDisplay only. Uses B4's chained translation factors.

S23

Pitcher models, fit separately from hitters: hazard model for P(MLB) and ETA, plus E[WAR in first 6 MLB seasons | reached] with rank-based model selection and the residual-scale variance model.newFeatures: MLEs, mERA (if it passes C15), age vs level, highest level, throwing hand, role (GS share, IP per game, pitches per outing), trajectory, progression pace, height and weight.

S24

P(MLB starter): logistic model on MiLB role features and MLEs, trained on 2016–2025 debuts. A pitcher counts as a starter if most of his appearances in his first two MLB seasons were starts.newreviewShown on the card for context only. Realized role is already in the WAR target. Role is known at debut, so this model can learn from modern bullpen usage.

S25

Eligibility: 150+ batters faced at A or above over the last 2 seasons (3 if under 20 IP in the snapshot season), and under 50 MLB IP. Everyone else gets a pitcher-specific draft prior (slot, bonus, high school vs college), flagged low confidence.new

S26

Injury flag on the card from injured-list placements in the snapshot season.newDisplay only. The target already prices average attrition.

S27

Arsenal table on tracked pitchers' cards (AAA 2023+, Low-A FSL 2021+): usage, velocity, spin, extension, and whiff% by pitch family.newDisplay only. Comes from grouped Savant CSVs, so no pitch-level downloads.

S28

Tracking contact adjustment: for tracked pitchers, blend Savant xwOBA-on-contact allowed into the contact term by sample size before mERA and the models.newgatedShips only if it passes C17.

S29

Two-way players: anyone eligible in both pipelines gets both projections, and board value is their sum, labeled "if both paths stick". Hitter-seasons logged as a pitcher stay out of hitter training.new

S30

Combined site: one leaderboard ranked by surplus $ with an All / Hitters / Pitchers filter and a role column. Pitcher cards mirror hitter cards, plus the mERA ladder, P(SP), arsenal, and IL flag. The methodology page adds a pitcher section, the split sensitivity, and the drift test.new

S31

2026 offseason snapshot rerun for hitters and pitchers, and published.

Approach

A17

A shared league-wide anchor is what makes cross-position WAR comparable.590/410 matches bWAR, which v1 already uses for validation and fielding.

A18

mERA is an input, not the target.Its job is forecasting. The 6-year WAR target stays unregressed so real contact management gets paid for. mERA stays as a feature only if it beats the raw components.

A19

Era drift since 2005 (K% up, velocity up, fewer starter innings, the pitch clock) is absorbed by league-season-relative MLEs, per-season anchoring, per-appearance SP/RP replacement, censored modern rows in the hazard, and P(SP) trained on modern debuts.Then tested rather than assumed: forward-chained CV (C18) and the 2018–19 holdout (C20).

A20

The WAR model trains on the latest complete-window snapshots (about s ≤ 2017, or the cutoff where under 2% of outcomes are censored), chosen on s ≤ 2012 data.

A21

The mERA Bayesian fit runs in Python inside the pipeline; library per Q22.

Done means

C13

Every season 2005–2026, position players sum to 590 WAR and pitchers to 410 (2020 prorated), within 1 WAR.review

C14

Pitcher WAR vs bWAR pitching: r ≥ 0.75 on MLB pitcher-seasons with 100+ IP. Reliever subset reported.reviewLower bar than hitters' 0.85 because ours is FIP-based and bWAR is runs-allowed-based. Gaps documented.

C15

mERA gate: kept as a model feature only if it improves s ≤ 2012 out-of-fold P(MLB) log loss or WAR Spearman without worsening the other. Varying slopes kept only if they beat fixed slopes on held-out next-season MLE RA9.Otherwise display only, and the null result is reported.

C16

Level-step test for pitchers: K%, BB%, GB% MLE changes for movers vs same-level repeaters within ±1 SE at A→A+, A+→AA, AA→AAA.

C17

Tracking gate: on AAA 2023–2025, blended contact predicts next-season contact allowed with lower RMSE than observed.If it fails, S28 is dropped and the null result is reported.

C18

Drift check: forward-chained CV (train ≤ 2009, test 2010–12) vs random folds on s ≤ 2012, both reported.

C19

Split sensitivity: how the combined 2026 top 50 changes at 57/43 and 61/39, reported on the methodology page.

C20

One holdout look for pitchers: 2013–17 vs MLB Pipeline top-100 pitchers and a naive age-vs-level + K−BB% baseline, plus 2018–19. Reported whichever way it goes. The combined board reports one Spearman across hitters and pitchers.

C21

Hitter restatement: hitter holdout metrics re-reported after re-anchoring (2013–17 look #6, 2018–19 look #2), with no other hitter change.

C22

v1's C5 (traceability, one-command rebuild), C6 (smell test), and C7 (phone width) apply to pitcher cards and the combined board.

Assumptions and open questions

Q19

The gmLI column in war_daily_pitch is verified in B17.If it's missing, relievers get no leverage credit and the methodology page says so.

Q20

The SP/RP replacement gap is fixed at the FanGraphs convention (about .380 vs .470 win%). Only the overall level is solved per season.

Q21

mERA target rows need 100+ batters faced in the next season, weighted by BF.

Q22

Bayesian library for mERA: PyMC (a new pinned dependency) or an empirical-Bayes approximation in existing dependencies. Decided in B19 before any fit.Partially pooled varying slopes need a real hierarchical fit.

Q23

The two-way sum overstates players whose careers end up on one path. Stated on the card and the methodology page.

Q24

Restating hitters changes every published hitter value, including figures already cited. Hold new citations until v2 publishes.

Q25

Pitcher translation pairs use 300 batters faced, mirroring v1's 300 PA.

Pre-registration

Committed before any pitcher model is fit. Every pitcher choice is made on s ≤ 2012 CV, and both pitcher holdouts are opened once, in B23. Expected directions:

  • Re-anchoring raises 2021–2026 hitter WAR (v1 sums 545–591) and lowers 2014–15 (638–639). Training-era hitter targets fall about 4% on average. 2026 hitter rank order barely moves (Spearman with current values ≥ 0.98).
  • The age-vs-level + K−BB% baseline is hard to beat. The model's margin over it should be smaller than v1's hitter margin over age + OPS.
  • mERA (C15) and the tracking adjustment (C17) may fail their gates. Either outcome gets reported.
  • Forward-chained CV scores worse than random folds. The gap measures drift.
  • Pitcher P(MLB) at a given level and age is lower than hitters', and the 10–90 WAR range is wider.
